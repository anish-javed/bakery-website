About & contact

A little bakery with a big sweet tooth

Crumbs & Co. started with one oven, one big bag of flour and a Lahore family that could not stop baking. Today we make cakes, hot patties, pizzas, soft bread and so much more, every single morning.

We use real butter, fresh milk and no shortcuts. Every birthday cake is baked to order, and every patty comes out of the oven warm.
